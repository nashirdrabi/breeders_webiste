Delete Account - BreedersHub

At BreedersHub, we value your privacy and give users complete control over their data.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account directly within the app:

Account → Delete Account

What happens after account deletion?

  • Your account will be permanently deleted.
  • Your profile information will be removed.
  • Your login credentials will be deleted.
  • Your associated data stored by BreedersHub will be removed.

Important information

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Some limited information may be retained temporarily for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or billing purposes.
